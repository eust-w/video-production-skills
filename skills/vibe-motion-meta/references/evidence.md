## Research snapshot

The initial research occurred on 2026-11-02 (Asia/Shanghai). These counts describe that phase only:

- 51 public repository records for [sxhzju](https://github.com/sxhzju) and 18 for [vibe-motion](https://github.com/vibe-motion), 69 total. Association evidence came from public GitHub information and the [Bonjour profile](https://bonjour.bio/dh171o).
- 59 root READMEs and one organization-profile README retrieved. Representative documents/selected source from 15 projects studied closely; this was not an audit of all source code.
- Descriptions for 16 collection skills reviewed; eight representative skills, including skills in other projects, read in full.
- 19 Bilibili, 19 Douyin, and five YouTube video links, with cross-platform duplicates; ten additional historical Bilibili index candidates recorded separately.
- 15 named platform chapter points across four videos; one platform-generated summary included the ending, while another covered only the middle.
- Zero complete verbatim transcripts and zero complete audiovisual viewings in this initial phase. Xiaohongshu account/post counts were confirmed, but zero post bodies read. This cannot support claiming familiarity with all video scripts.

Platform-generated summaries may paraphrase or mistranscribe; they are not verbatim quotations. Titles can inform title-packaging analysis, but do not prove one-prompt, zero-cost, or fully automated production. Avoid inventing catchphrases, speaking speeds, habitual phrase frequencies, or fixed editing templates.
